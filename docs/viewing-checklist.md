# 🔍 Viewing checklist

Print this (or open it on your phone) when you go see an apartment or house. Split the list: one of you checks the **space**, the other checks the **systems**, and then you swap notes over coffee.

**Address:** ______________________ **Date:** ________ **Price / rent:** ________

## First impressions (fill in separately, then compare)

| Favorite thing                   |           |           |

## The space

- [ ] Natural light: which way do the main windows face? Visit at different times of day if you can.
- [ ] Does our sofa / bed / dining table actually fit? Bring a tape measure, then sketch it in **Gougou House → Floor Plans**.
- [ ] Storage: closets, pantry, a place for the vacuum, suitcases and the dog stuff
- [ ] Where would each of us work from home?
- [ ] Noise: neighbors, street, elevator, bars downstairs. Stand still for 30 seconds and listen.
- [ ] Ceiling height, and any awkward corners or columns
- [ ] Cell signal and Wi-Fi in every room

## The systems

- [ ] Water pressure and hot water: run the shower **and** a faucet at the same time
- [ ] Heating / AC type, and roughly what the bills run in winter and summer
- [ ] Signs of damp or mold: under sinks, window frames, bathroom ceiling
- [ ] Windows open, close and lock properly
- [ ] Outlets: enough of them, and any USB ones
- [ ] Laundry: in-unit, in-building, or the laundromat down the street?
- [ ] Appliances: age and condition (fridge, stove, dishwasher)

## The building & neighborhood

- [ ] Package handling, building security, entry system
- [ ] Trash and recycling: where and how often
- [ ] Commute for **both** of us at rush hour (check maps on the spot)
- [ ] Groceries, coffee, park, gym, vet 🐶 within walking distance
- [ ] Pet policy, written down: size limits, fees, deposit
- [ ] Parking or bike storage
- [ ] Walk around the block at night

## Money & lease

- [ ] Total monthly cost: rent or mortgage + utilities + fees + parking (put it in **Gougou House → Budget**)
- [ ] Deposit, broker fee, move-in fees
- [ ] Lease length, renewal terms, rent increase history
- [ ] Who to call when something breaks, and how fast they actually come
- [ ] For buyers: HOA fees, special assessments, property tax, age of the roof and boiler

## Questions to ask

1. Why are the current tenants / owners leaving?
2. What are average utility costs?
3. Has anything been repaired recently?
4. Are there any planned renovations in the building?
5. How flexible is the move-in date?

> 🐶 Gougou's rule: if you're both still smiling on the walk back to the car, that's a good sign.
